| Temple exit portal and limestone grain | Original geometry and texture generated by `tools/blender/temple_exit.py`. Godot extracts the embedded limestone image during GLB import. |
| Temple ambience and effects | Original synthesized WAV assets for the room, tremors, box lid, bridge impact, and victory cue. |
| Screenshots and model renders | Captured from Pandora in Godot or rendered from its original models in Blender. The `art_previews/` images are included as portfolio documentation. |
